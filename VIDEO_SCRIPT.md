# UNICEF Femtech pitch video — recording script

**Length:** 90–110 seconds. **Format:** a real Primed team member speaking on camera, intercut with a live screen recording of the working CarePath prototype. Add English subtitles if needed. Do not use AI avatars, synthetic voices, or generated footage.

## Opening — team member on camera (0:00–0:20)

“I’m [name], [role] at Primed E-Health in Nigeria. For many women, a pregnancy can involve several clinics, repeated paperwork, missed referrals and follow-up that gets lost when care moves between facilities. That makes it harder for care teams to see the next step in a woman’s journey.”

## Product in action — live demo capture (0:20–1:10)

Show the fictional Amina Sani demo record. Select the antenatal visit, confirm the appointment, open the referral status, and demonstrate the patient-controlled information-sharing switches. Keep the fictional-data label visible.

“CarePath is our maternal continuity prototype. It gives a woman and her care team one simple view of her care journey: the next antenatal visit, a referral to another facility, and whether that referral was received. Amina’s record here is fictional. The sharing controls make clear that the patient chooses what is shared with each participating provider. The design supports assisted access and short, low-bandwidth workflows.”

## Close — team member on camera (1:10–1:40)

“We are preparing this as a separate open-source component so local health teams can inspect and adapt the workflow. The prototype is not yet connected to live patient records. With UNICEF’s support, we want to validate it with women and frontline providers, complete privacy and safety testing, integrate approved referral pathways, and evaluate whether follow-up becomes more reliable across community and tertiary care.”

## Recording checklist

- Use a real team member and a real capture of the prototype running in a browser.
- State the speaker’s name and role accurately; obtain their agreement to publish.
- Keep all data fictional; do not show real patient names, phone numbers, diagnoses, or records.
- Record in English or add human-reviewed English subtitles.
- Publish on YouTube as unlisted and paste the resulting URL into the UNICEF form.
- Keep total runtime under two minutes.
